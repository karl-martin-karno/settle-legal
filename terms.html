About Erana

What Erana does, and what it does not

  • Erana records expenses and payments that you and your group enter, and calculates each person's share and balance from them.
  • Erana does not move money. Payments are made outside the app, for example in cash or by bank transfer. When you record a payment, you are telling the group that it happened.
  • Erana does not give financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Savings goals, daily amounts and reminders are only a help to keep track, not advice.
  • A group can have expenses in more than one currency. Erana keeps a separate balance for each currency and does not convert between them.
  • Balances and suggestions are only as correct as the information that you and your group enter. Check them before you rely on them.

Your account

  • You must be at least 16 years old to use Erana.
  • If you are under 18, you need the permission of a parent or guardian to buy Premium.
  • Keep your sign-in details safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Give correct information. Do not pretend to be someone else, and do not add a phone number that is not yours.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. The privacy policy explains what is kept and what is removed.

Your content

  • You are responsible for the text and amounts that you enter, such as expense descriptions and notes.
  • Do not enter content that is illegal, abusive, or harmful, and do not put other people's private details in expense descriptions or notes.
  • You keep the rights to what you enter. You give Erana a free, non-exclusive licence to store, copy and show it to the people you share it with, only as far as needed to run Erana. The licence ends when the content is deleted, except for the shared records that are kept as described in the privacy policy.
  • Other members of your groups can see the expenses, payments, and notes in those groups. Text you wrote in expense descriptions stays visible to the group after you leave or delete your account, as described in the privacy policy.

The assistant

The assistant uses an AI model to read receipts, fill in expenses and answer questions. An AI can misread a receipt, leave out items, or calculate a wrong amount, split or balance. Everything the assistant fills in is a suggestion. You must check every value before you save it, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. Once you save an expense, it is your entry, as if you had typed it yourself.

The assistant's answers about your groups and spending are for information only. The amounts in the app's lists and balances are what count. The assistant does not give financial, tax or legal advice, and it never moves money. Do not write passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information to it.

Debts between users

Debts and payments in Erana are between you and the other people in your groups or friendships. The operator of Erana is not a party to them, does not collect debts, and does not decide who is right in a disagreement. A balance in Erana is a calculation from what members entered. Erana does not intend it as a promise to pay or an acknowledgement of debt, and does not verify that any entry is true. We do not confirm any balance to you, to other users or to anyone else. If you disagree with an entry, settle it with the other members. Every member of the group or friendship can see the history of changes. We do not hand over group or friendship data to settle a private dispute unless the law requires us to.
