About Erana

Erana is a bill-splitting app. It helps a group keep track of shared costs and who owes whom. The operator of Erana is Karl-Martin Karnö, contactable at privacy@erana.eu. Postal address: Honungsgatan 38
75255 Uppsala
Sweden.

By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use Erana.

What Erana does, and what it does not

  • Erana records expenses and payments that you and your group enter, and calculates each person's share and balance from them.
  • Erana does not move money. Payments are made outside the app, for example in cash or by bank transfer. When you record a payment, you are telling the group that it happened.
  • Erana does not give financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Savings goals, daily amounts and reminders are only a help to keep track, not advice.
  • A group can have expenses in more than one currency. Erana keeps a separate balance for each currency and does not convert between them.
  • Balances and suggestions are only as correct as the information that you and your group enter. Check them before you rely on them.

Your account

  • You must be at least 16 years old to use Erana.
  • Keep your sign-in details safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Give correct information. Do not pretend to be someone else, and do not add a phone number that is not yours.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. The privacy policy explains what is kept and what is removed.

Your content

  • You are responsible for the text and amounts that you enter, such as expense descriptions and notes.
  • Do not enter content that is illegal, abusive, or harmful, and do not put other people's private details in expense descriptions or notes.
  • Other members of your groups can see the expenses, payments, and notes in those groups. Text you wrote in expense descriptions stays visible to the group after you leave or delete your account, as described in the privacy policy.

The assistant

  • The assistant in Erana is an AI system. It can make mistakes, so check every value it fills in before you save, and check its answers against the app.
  • You confirm every expense before it is saved. Nothing is saved without you.
  • Do not write passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information to the assistant.
  • The assistant does not give financial advice and never moves money.
