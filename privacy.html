Contacts

When there is no connection, an expense you add is saved in a file in the Erana app's own storage on your phone until it can be sent, together with a copy of your groups, their members and the categories so the form works without a connection. This file is not sent anywhere except the expense itself, when it is sent to Erana. It is deleted when you sign out and when you delete your account.

With your permission, Erana reads the names and phone numbers in your phone's contacts on your device. Only the phone numbers are sent to Erana, to check which people have an account. Erana does not store your contacts. Names from your contacts stay on your device, in the app's memory, and are cleared when you close the app.

You can refuse contacts access, or change it later in your phone's settings.

Sign-in

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, with Google, or with Apple. Google and Apple send us the information you agree to share with Erana. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and hosting (Stockholm, EU).
  • Sentry: crash reports, without personal data.
  • Enable Banking Oy (an open-banking provider, Finland, EU): only if you connect a bank. You log in at your bank and approve read-only access. Enable Banking passes your bank's answer to Erana's server, so Erana can read the account and transactions you approved. Erana can never make payments or change anything at your bank. Your bank login details are never seen by Erana. Your access ends after a limited time (at most 90 days, depending on the bank) or when you remove the bank in Settings. When you remove a bank, or delete your account, Erana first closes the connection at the bank, so it no longer shows as active there. If that cannot be done, the bank is not removed and the account is not deleted until it can be. Bank connections are not offered to testers yet. They will not be offered to other users before an agreement with Enable Banking is in place.
  • Expo and Apple: if you allow notifications, the title and text of each notification, and your device token, are sent through Expo's push service and Apple's push notification service to reach your phone. Erana puts no amounts or expense descriptions in the text.
  • Google and Apple: only when you choose to sign in with them.

Other group members can see your display name, your balances in groups you share, and the payments and expenses in those groups. Your friends can see your display name, your profile photo, and the expenses and payments between you. A friend is added at once when someone uses your friend code, or your phone number from their contacts, without asking you first.

How long we keep data

Data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account from the Profile screen at any time. When you do:

  • Your sign-in, email address, phone number hash, private settings, notification settings, device tokens, queued notifications, friends, shared expenses with friends and their history, invites, income entries, your Swish number, your IBAN, expenses of your own with their earlier versions, repeating expenses, budgets, bank connections, imported bank transactions, and custom categories (for expenses and income) are deleted.
  • Receipt photos you added to group expenses, your profile photo, your savings goals, and any tester reports with their screenshots, are deleted.
  • Your name is replaced with "Deleted user". The expenses and payments you took part in stay in your groups, with your identity removed. The ledger is append-only, and other members' balances depend on it, so these records cannot be deleted without making their balances wrong.
  • Text you wrote stays visible to the other members of your groups: expense descriptions, the names of items, and the earlier versions of an expense that its history keeps, with the items each version had. Notes you wrote on payments and payment requests are cleared. Do not put personal details in expense descriptions.
  • If you were the only admin of a group, the member who joined first becomes admin.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export, and delete your data:

  • See and correct: your name, currency, country, and other profile details are on the Profile screen.
  • Export: the Profile screen can export a copy of your data as JSON, including your bank connections and imported transactions, your own categories and your notification settings, the earlier versions of your own expenses, your cover rules and the covers of each expense that involve you, including when you agreed to the terms, and your expenses and payments as CSV. Other people appear by display name only, where a record involves them.
  • Delete: see "How long we keep data" above.

You can also contact us at privacy@erana.eu.

Security

Data is stored in a database with row-level security, so each person can only read the rows they are allowed to see. Money amounts are stored as whole numbers. The sign-in session is kept in the secure storage of your phone, the iPhone Keychain or the Android Keystore. On an iPhone it stays on that phone only: it is not included in backups or moved to a new phone. Bank access is read-only, and the key that Erana uses to talk to the bank provider is kept only on Erana's server, never in the app. Bank texts, amounts and codes are never written to logs. You can turn on an app lock in Settings, which asks for Face ID or your passcode when the app opens. The check is done by iOS on your phone: Erana never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data, and the lock setting stays on your phone. A group can have at most 50 members. Each person can check up to 1,000 phone numbers a day. When a number belongs to an account, the person who checks it sees that account's name. Requests that could be used to guess codes or numbers are rate limited.

Children

Erana is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this document when the data we collect or how we use it changes, and we will tell users in the app when that happens.
