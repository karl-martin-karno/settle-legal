Contacts

With your permission, Erana reads the names and phone numbers in your phone's contacts on your device. Only the phone numbers are sent to Erana, to check which people have an account. Erana does not store your contacts. Names from your contacts stay on your device, in the app's memory, and are cleared when you close the app.

You can refuse contacts access, or change it later in the iOS Settings app. Friends can still be added some other way once that is available.

Sign-in

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, with Google, or with Apple. Google and Apple send us the information you agree to share with Erana. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and hosting (Stockholm, EU).
  • Sentry: crash reports, without personal data.
  • Enable Banking Oy (an open-banking provider, Finland, EU): only if you connect a bank. You log in at your bank and approve read-only access. Enable Banking passes your bank's answer to Erana's server, so Erana can read the account and transactions you approved. Erana can never make payments or change anything at your bank. Your bank login details are never seen by Erana. Your access ends after a limited time (at most 90 days, depending on the bank) or when you remove the bank in Settings.   • Expo and Apple: if you allow notifications, the title and text of each notification, and your device token, are sent through Expo's push service and Apple's push notification service to reach your phone. Erana puts no amounts or expense descriptions in the text.
  • Google and Apple: only when you choose to sign in with them.

Other group members can see your display name, your balances in groups you share, and the payments and expenses in those groups.

How long we keep data

Data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account from the Profile screen at any time. When you do:

  • Your sign-in, email address, phone number hash, private settings, notification settings, device tokens, queued notifications, friends, invites, income entries, bank connections, imported bank transactions, and custom categories (for expenses and income) are deleted.
  • Your name is replaced with "Deleted user". The expenses and payments you took part in stay in your groups, with your identity removed. The ledger is append-only, and other members' balances depend on it, so these records cannot be deleted without making their balances wrong.
  • Text you wrote stays visible to the other members of your groups: expense descriptions, and the earlier versions of an expense that its history keeps. Notes you wrote on payments and payment requests are cleared. Do not put personal details in expense descriptions.
  • If you were the only admin of a group, the member who joined first becomes admin.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export, and delete your data:

  • See and correct: your name, currency, country, accent color, and other profile details are on the Profile screen.
  • Export: the Profile screen can export a copy of your data as JSON, including your bank connections and imported transactions, including when you agreed to the terms, and your expenses and payments as CSV. Other people appear by display name only, where a record involves them.
  • Delete: see "How long we keep data" above.

You can also contact us at [contact email to be added before launch].

Security

Data is stored in a database with row-level security, so each person can only read the rows they are allowed to see. Money amounts are stored as whole numbers. The sign-in session is kept in the iPhone Keychain, on that phone only: it is not included in backups or moved to a new phone. Bank access is read-only, and the key that Erana uses to talk to the bank provider is kept only on Erana's server, never in the app. Bank texts, amounts and codes are never written to logs. You can turn on an app lock in Settings, which asks for Face ID or your passcode when the app opens. The check is done by iOS on your phone: Erana never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data, and the lock setting stays on your phone. A group can have at most 50 members, and each person can look up phone numbers a limited number of times a day, so the lookup cannot be used to scan for numbers. Requests that could be used to guess codes or numbers are rate limited.

Children

Erana is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this document when the data we collect or how we use it changes, and we will tell users in the app when that happens.
