Everything else is optional or is used only when you choose a feature. Other members of your groups can add you to expenses and record payments that involve you.

Legal bases

We process your data on these grounds under Article 6(1) of the GDPR:

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract, Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, profile, groups, expenses, payments, friends, income, budgets, savings goals, notifications, your Swish number and IBAN when you add them, the AI assistant when you turn it on (after you agree to its notice), and Premium.
  • Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): security, rate limiting and preventing abuse; crash reports to fix errors; checking phone numbers from your contacts against our accounts; keeping shared expense records after an account is deleted, so other members' balances stay correct; and keeping a record that you accepted these documents. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests (see "Your rights").
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping records that we must keep by law.

Contacts

When there is no connection, an expense you add is saved in a file in the Erana app's own storage on your phone until it can be sent, together with a copy of your groups, their members and the categories so the form works without a connection. This file is not sent anywhere except the expense itself, when it is sent to Erana. It is deleted when you sign out and when you delete your account.

You can turn off "Let people find me by phone number" in Settings, under Find me. When it is off, people who check their contacts cannot find you, and are not told that your number has an account. Your friend code, QR code and group invites still work.

With your permission, Erana reads the names and phone numbers in your phone's contacts on your device. Only the phone numbers are sent to Erana, to check which people have an account. Erana answers only which numbers have an account, not who they are: the name you see is the one in your own contacts. Erana does not store your contacts. The phone numbers that Erana receives from your contacts are compared with our records and then discarded. We do not store numbers that do not belong to an Erana account, and we do not build a list of people who are not users. The phone number of a user is stored as a keyed one-way hash (and, for a Swedish mobile number, also as the Swish number described above), which is still personal data under the GDPR and is protected and deleted as such. Names from your contacts stay on your device, in the app's memory, and are cleared when you close the app.

You can refuse contacts access, or change it later in your phone's settings.

Sign-in

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, with Google, or with Apple. Google and Apple send us the information you agree to share with Erana. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and hosting (Stockholm, EU).
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., United States; reports are stored in the EU): crash reports with technical data only, set up not to include your name, email or phone number.
  • RevenueCat: manages Premium subscriptions with Apple and Google, and receives the purchase events that Erana records. It gets your Erana account ID, not your name or email. It stores and processes data in the United States, on Amazon Web Services. Transfers are covered by RevenueCat's data processing addendum. The transfer mechanism is still to be confirmed by the lawyer before this policy is published.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: if you allow notifications, the title and text of each notification and your device token are sent through Expo's push service (Expo, United States) and then through Apple's push notification service (iPhone) or Google's push service (Android) to reach your phone. Erana puts no amounts or expense descriptions in the text.
  • Google and Apple: only when you choose to sign in with them.

Other group members can see your display name, your balances in groups you share, and the payments and expenses in those groups. If another member of your group, or a friend, uses the AI assistant, your display name and the amounts, balances and recent expenses in that group or between the two of you may be sent to our AI provider to answer their question. You can object to this by writing to privacy@erana.eu. Your friends can see your display name, your profile photo, and the expenses and payments between you. A friendship is made only when the person who is asked accepts a friend request. Someone who uses your friend code, or finds your phone number in their contacts, can only send you a request. You see their display name in the request, and they see nothing about you until you accept.

How long we keep data

Data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account from the Profile screen at any time. When you do:

  • Your sign-in, email address, phone number hash, private settings, notification settings, device tokens, queued notifications, friend requests and blocks, invites, income entries, your Swish number, your IBAN, expenses of your own with their earlier versions, repeating expenses, budgets, any bank connection data left from earlier test versions of the app (if any exists for your account), and custom categories (for expenses and income) are deleted.
  • Receipt photos you added to group expenses, your profile photo, your savings goals, and any tester reports with their screenshots, are deleted.
  • Your friendships with no outstanding balance are removed. A friendship with an outstanding balance stays until settled. Shared expenses with friends, their earlier versions and ledger, and recorded payments stay so the other person can read the history and their balance stays correct.
  • Your name is replaced with "Deleted user". The expenses and payments you took part in stay in your groups, with your name removed. Other members who knew you may still recognise you from them. The ledger is append-only, and other members' balances depend on it, so these records cannot be deleted without making their balances wrong.
  • Text you wrote stays visible to the other members of your groups: expense descriptions, the names of items, and the earlier versions of an expense that its history keeps, with the items each version had. Notes you wrote on payments and payment requests are cleared. Do not put personal details in expense descriptions.
  • If you were the only admin of a group, the member who joined first becomes admin.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Where we rely on your agreement, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • See and correct: your name, currency, country, and other profile details are on the Profile screen.
  • Export: the Profile screen can export a copy of your data as JSON, including any bank connection data left from earlier test versions of the app (if any exists for your account), your own categories and your notification settings, the earlier versions of your own expenses, your cover rules and the covers of each expense that involve you, including when you agreed to the terms, and your expenses and payments as CSV. Other people appear by display name only, where a record involves them.
  • Delete: see "How long we keep data" above.

For anything else, write to privacy@erana.eu. We answer within one month.

You can complain to a data protection authority: in Sweden, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY, imy.se); in Estonia, Andmekaitse Inspektsioon (aki.ee); or the authority where you live or work.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI assistant only suggests values; you decide what is saved.

Security

Data is stored in a database with row-level security, so each person can only read the rows they are allowed to see. Money amounts are stored as whole numbers. The sign-in session is kept in the secure storage of your phone, the iPhone Keychain or the Android Keystore. On an iPhone it stays on that phone only: it is not included in backups or moved to a new phone. You can turn on an app lock in Settings, which asks for Face ID or your passcode when the app opens. The check is done by iOS on your phone: Erana never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data, and the lock setting stays on your phone. A group can have at most 50 members. Each person can check up to 1,000 phone numbers a day. When a number belongs to an account, the person who checks it learns only that the number has an account, not the account's name. Requests that could be used to guess codes or numbers are rate limited.

Children

Erana is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this document when the data we collect or how we use it changes, and we will tell users in the app when that happens.
