Contacts

With your permission, Settle reads the names and phone numbers in your phone's contacts on your device. Only the phone numbers are sent to Settle, to check which people have an account. Settle does not store your contacts. Names from your contacts stay on your device, in the app's memory, and are cleared when you close the app.

You can refuse contacts access, or change it later in the iOS Settings app. Friends can still be added some other way once that is available.

Sign-in

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, with Google, or with Apple. Google and Apple send us the information you agree to share with Settle. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and hosting (Stockholm, EU).
  • Sentry: crash reports, without personal data.
  • Google and Apple: only when you choose to sign in with them.

Other group members can see your display name, your balances in groups you share, and the payments and expenses in those groups.

How long we keep data

Data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account from the Profile screen at any time. When you do:

  • Your sign-in, email address, phone number hash, friends, invites, and custom categories are deleted.
  • Your name is replaced with "Deleted user". The expenses and payments you took part in stay in your groups, with your identity removed. The ledger is append-only, and other members' balances depend on it, so these records cannot be deleted without making their balances wrong.
  • Text you wrote stays visible to the other members of your groups: expense descriptions, and the earlier versions of an expense that its history keeps. Notes you wrote on payments and payment requests are cleared. Do not put personal details in expense descriptions.
  • If you were the only admin of a group, the member who joined first becomes admin.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export, and delete your data:

  • See and correct: your name, currency, and other profile details are on the Profile screen.
  • Export: the Profile screen can export a copy of your data as JSON, and your expenses and payments as CSV. Other people appear by display name only, where a record involves them.
  • Delete: see "How long we keep data" above.

You can also contact us at [contact email to be added before launch].

Security

Data is stored in a database with row-level security, so each person can only read the rows they are allowed to see. Money amounts are stored as whole numbers. The sign-in session is kept in the iPhone Keychain. Requests that could be used to guess codes or numbers are rate limited.

Children

Settle is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this document when the data we collect or how we use it changes, and we will tell users in the app when that happens.
