Bill splitting for groups

Erana

Split bills with friends without the awkward maths.

Erana is a bill-splitting app for groups of friends, flatmates and travellers. It is built in Sweden and comes to iPhone first, for Sweden and Estonia.

The idea

Shared costs are easy to lose track of. Someone pays for the groceries, someone else for the rent, someone for the dinner, and a month later nobody is sure who owes whom.

Erana keeps one clear record for the group. Everyone adds what they paid, and every member sees the same balance. When it is time to settle up, Erana suggests the fewest payments that clear everything, so nobody has to do the maths or send an awkward reminder.

  • Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentages, by shares, or with adjustments such as an extra drink for one person.
  • One balance for the whole group, the same for everyone.
  • Payments are made outside the app, in cash, by bank transfer or with Swish, and recorded in Erana.
  • No ads, and you can always export your own data.

What makes Erana different

Most apps do one half of the job. Some split bills with friends, others track your own money. Erana is built so the two halves sit side by side, and so that the private half stays private.

  • Your money and the shared money in one place. Your own income, your own expenses and your budgets sit next to the costs you share, so you see what you actually spend in a month, not only what you owe.
  • Your bank, on your terms. You can bring in your own spending and income from your bank. You log in at your bank, Erana can only read, and every payment waits in an inbox until you decide what it is: your own spending, income, something to split with a group, or something to ignore. Nothing counts until you have sorted it, and a payment that is already in a group is linked to it, so it is never counted twice.
  • Private stays private. Your income, budgets, own expenses and bank payments are visible only to you. They are never shown to the members of your groups.
  • Repeating costs without surprises. Rent and subscriptions can repeat on their own. A repeating cost in a group waits for you to confirm it each time, so nothing lands on other people's balances without a person looking at it first.
  • Cover a friend. One person can choose to cover another person's share in a group, for a whole trip or for good. It is shown openly to the group, it only applies to new expenses, and it can be ended at any time.
  • Made for Sweden and Estonia. Swedish kronor and euro, Swish that opens with the amount already filled in when you settle up, and your data stored in the EU.
  • Your data is yours. No ads, no selling of data, and a full export of everything stored about you, whenever you want it.

Why I am building it

I am building Erana to make my own life easier. Every app I tried was missing something, so I want everything in one app: my own income and spending, costs shared with friends, and settling up, with as little typing as possible.

Your data

Your data is stored in the EU. Erana does not sell it, and it asks for your contacts only if you choose to find friends, and then only checks phone numbers without keeping your contact list. If you connect a bank, you log in at your bank, Erana can only read, and what it reads is visible only to you.

What is ready, and what is coming

Working in the app today

  • Groups, expense splitting, balances and settling up.
  • Income, repeating costs, budgets with warnings, and covering a friend's share.
  • Swish for groups in kronor: Erana opens the Swish app with the other member's number and the amount already filled in. You pay with BankID and record the payment in Erana.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code, and an optional app lock with Face ID.
  • Export of your data.
  • Bank import, currently tested only on my own accounts.

Planned, but not available yet

  • Trips with several currencies, reading receipts, and an assistant that helps you add expenses by writing.
  • Paying a friend from inside Erana. This needs a payment partner and a legal review first, so there is no date for it.
  • Opening other banks' payment screens with the amount already filled in, where the bank allows it.
  • A decision on which parts of Erana will be free and which will be paid.

Erana is still in development. An early test version for a small group in Sweden and Estonia is planned before the public launch.
