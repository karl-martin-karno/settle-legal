Bill splitting for groups

Erana

Split bills with friends without the awkward maths.

Erana is a bill-splitting app for groups of friends, flatmates and travellers. It is built in Sweden and comes to iPhone first, for Sweden and Estonia.

The idea

Shared costs are easy to lose track of. Someone pays for the groceries, someone else for the rent, someone for the dinner, and a month later nobody is sure who owes whom.

Erana keeps one clear record for the group. Everyone adds what they paid, and every member sees the same balance. When it is time to settle up, Erana suggests the fewest payments that clear everything, so nobody has to do the maths or send an awkward reminder.

  • Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentages or by shares.
  • One balance for the whole group, the same for everyone.
  • Payments are made outside the app, in cash or by bank transfer, and recorded in Erana.
  • No ads, and you can always export your own data.

Why I am building it

I am building Erana to make my own life easier. Every app I tried was missing something, so I want everything in one app: my own spending, costs shared with friends, and settling up.

Your data

Your data is stored in the EU. Erana does not sell it, and it asks for your contacts only if you choose to find friends, and then only checks phone numbers without keeping your contact list.

Status

Erana is still in development. An early test version for a small group in Sweden and Estonia is planned before the public launch.
